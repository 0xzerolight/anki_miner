kiwipiepy - Korean morphological analyser (LGPL-3.0-or-later)
Upstream source: https://github.com/bab2min/kiwipiepy
Model data (kiwipiepy_model): https://github.com/bab2min/kiwipiepy_model

The binaries this application downloads are the unmodified PyPI artifacts for
kiwipiepy 0.23.2 and kiwipiepy_model 0.23.0. Those versions are not resolved at
build time: they are pinned, with their SHA-256 digests, in
anki_miner/languages/ko/pack.py. Corresponding source for the exact versions
downloaded is available from the URLs above and from PyPI:
  https://pypi.org/project/kiwipiepy/#files
  https://pypi.org/project/kiwipiepy-model/#files
