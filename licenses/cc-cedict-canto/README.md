# CC-CEDICT Canto

`anki_miner/languages/yue/data/simplified_only.txt` is derived from the
`CC-CEDICT.Canto.zip` Yomitan build of
[MarvNC/cc-cedict-yomitan](https://github.com/MarvNC/cc-cedict-yomitan), revision
2026-09-19: CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/, MDBG) with the Cantonese readings
Pleco publishes as CC-Canto (https://cccanto.org/). Its publisher releases the
dictionary under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0** licence that
CC-CEDICT is distributed under — the full text is in
[`LICENSE.CC-BY-SA-3.0`](LICENSE.CC-BY-SA-3.0). The derived file ships under the same
licence; the file's own header records the same provenance.

## What was derived

The characters that occur in the simplified headword span of a row whose two
spellings differ and in no traditional headword span anywhere in the dictionary:
2,160 characters, one per line, sorted by codepoint. No definitions, readings or
other entry text are carried.

## The dictionaries the application downloads

The CC-CEDICT and CC-Canto dictionaries in the Chinese and Cantonese catalogs are
downloaded on demand from upstream and never redistributed here.
