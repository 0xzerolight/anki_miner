fr_core_news_sm - spaCy French pipeline (LGPL-LR)
Release: https://github.com/explosion/spacy-models/releases/tag/fr_core_news_sm-3.8.0
Model source and training configuration: https://github.com/explosion/spacy-models

Training data, as listed in the model's own meta.json:
  UD French Sequoia v2.8 (LGPL-LR) - https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_French-Sequoia
  WikiNER (CC BY 4.0) - https://figshare.com/articles/Learning_multilingual_named_entity_recognition_from_Wikipedia/5462500
  spaCy lookups data (MIT) - https://github.com/explosion/spacy-lookups-data
The full texts of those licences are the wheel's own LICENSES_SOURCES file, which
stays beside the model data after the language pack is extracted
(~/.anki_miner/language_packs/fr/fr_core_news_sm/fr_core_news_sm-3.8.0/LICENSES_SOURCES).

The binary this application downloads is the unmodified wheel
fr_core_news_sm-3.8.0-py3-none-any.whl. Its URL and SHA-256 digest are pinned in
anki_miner/languages/fr/pack.py; corresponding source for that exact version is
available from the release and repository URLs above.
