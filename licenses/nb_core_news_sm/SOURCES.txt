nb_core_news_sm - spaCy Norwegian Bokmål pipeline (MIT)
Release: https://github.com/explosion/spacy-models/releases/tag/nb_core_news_sm-3.8.0
Model source and training configuration: https://github.com/explosion/spacy-models

Training data, as listed in the model's own meta.json:
  UD Norwegian Bokmaal v2.8 (Public Domain, CC0) - https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Norwegian-Bokmaal
  NorNE: Norwegian Named Entities, commit bd311de5 (Public Domain, CC0) - https://github.com/ltgoslo/norne
The full texts of those terms are the wheel's own LICENSES_SOURCES file, which
stays beside the model data after the language pack is extracted
(~/.anki_miner/language_packs/nb/nb_core_news_sm/nb_core_news_sm-3.8.0/LICENSES_SOURCES).

The binary this application downloads is the unmodified wheel
nb_core_news_sm-3.8.0-py3-none-any.whl. Its URL and SHA-256 digest are pinned in
anki_miner/languages/nb/pack.py; corresponding source for that exact version is
available from the release and repository URLs above.
