The full texts of those licences are the wheel's own LICENSES_SOURCES file, which
stays beside the model data after the language pack is extracted
(~/.anki_miner/language_packs/es/es_core_news_sm/es_core_news_sm-3.8.0/LICENSES_SOURCES).

The binary this application downloads is the unmodified wheel
es_core_news_sm-3.8.0-py3-none-any.whl. Its URL and SHA-256 digest are pinned in
anki_miner/languages/es/pack.py; corresponding source for that exact version is
available from the release and repository URLs above.
