The full text of those terms is the wheel's own LICENSES_SOURCES file, which
stays beside the model data after the language pack is extracted
(~/.anki_miner/language_packs/ru/ru_core_news_sm/ru_core_news_sm-3.8.0/LICENSES_SOURCES).

The binary this application downloads is the unmodified wheel
ru_core_news_sm-3.8.0-py3-none-any.whl. Its URL and SHA-256 digest are pinned in
anki_miner/languages/ru/pack.py; corresponding source for that exact version is
available from the release and repository URLs above.
